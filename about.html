Why we exist

Symptom care is everywhere.
Root-cause training wasn't.

India produces excellent nutritionists and doctors — but almost nowhere teaches them to work as functional practitioners: to read 300+ biomarkers as patterns, trace dysfunction upstream, and design protocols that fix causes instead of managing labels.

We built EverHealthy to be that place. Since 2018, our certification arc has grown from one foundations course to six sequenced credentials, eight condition masteries and a clinical team that heals patients directly — so our teaching never drifts from real practice.

Every case study in our classrooms comes from a real chart. Every framework is one our own clinicians use daily.
